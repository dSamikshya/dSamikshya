**A comprehensive enterprise-level inventory management REST API**

- ✅ Product & Category Management with Batch Tracking (FIFO)
- ✅ Customer Management with Credit Limit Tracking

- 4 User Roles with Granular Permissions
- 8+ Database Tables
- Complete CRUD Operations
- Bulk Operations Support
